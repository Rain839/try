Who we are

We are a group of students from St. Rose of Lima catholic school that is advocating Green ICT to make it easy to understand and easier to put it in practice

What we believe

Clarity over jargon

Sustainability reports are often impenetrable. We translate them into plain language anyone can act on.

Measure, then improve

You cannot cut what you do not count. We champion honest measurement of energy, carbon, and waste.

Longevity

The greenest device is the one you already own. We favour repair, reuse, and keeping things in service.

Open knowledge

Our research, notes, and articles are open for anyone to read and build upon.

Ten students, one project. Click on any of us to get our full contact details if you want to reach out directly.
