Green ICT touches nearly every part of how we make, use, and discard technology.

01

Energy & emissions

The ICT sector alone produces 2–3% of global carbon emissions.Cutting that starts with smarter power use.

 02

Electronic waste

Discarded devices are the world's fastest growing waste stream. Repair, reuse, and recycling are the cure.

 03

Device longevity

Most of a device's environmental cost comes from making it.Extending its life by even one year matters.

 04

Renewable energy

Solar panels and wind turbines generate clean electricity with zero fuel and zero emissions.

 05

Sustainable transport

Electric vehicles,public transit, cycling, and walking ways to move without leaving carbon behind.

 06

Waste-to-energy

Turning trash into electricity, heat,and fuel recovering value from what would otherwise be landfilled.

 07

Carbon capture

Trapping CO₂ straight from factory pipes or the air itself,then storing it or turning it into useful products.

 08

Green ICT in schools

How classrooms can save energy, cut paper, and teach the next generation to use technology responsibly.
