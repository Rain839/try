Your Green ICT action list

Print this page, stick it on your desk or beside your charger, and check off what you already do. The items you leave unchecked are your next steps.

Green ICT Student Checklist

Small habits, big difference. Tick the boxes you already do.

Power

  • Turn off my computer when I'm done using it
  • Enable sleep mode on my laptop after 5 minutes of inactivity
  • Unplug my phone charger when my phone is fully charged
  • Turn off the monitor when I step away from the desk
  • Use a power strip and switch it off at night

Paper

  • Take notes digitally instead of on paper
  • Submit assignments online when the option exists
  • Print double-sided only when printing is necessary
  • Read e-books or PDFs instead of printed handouts
  • Reuse the back of already-printed paper for scratch notes

Devices

  • Keep my phone for at least 3 years before upgrading
  • Use a case and screen protector to extend my phone's life
  • Repair a cracked screen or weak battery instead of replacing the whole device
  • Delete apps and files I don't use to keep my device running smoothly
  • Give away or sell old devices instead of leaving them in a drawer

E-waste

  • Never throw old batteries in the regular trash
  • Find my nearest e-waste recycling drop-off point
  • Drop off old chargers, cables, and earphones at a recycling center
  • Ask my school if they have an e-waste collection program
  • Tell at least one friend or family member about proper e-waste disposal

Daily habits

  • Close background tabs and apps I'm not using
  • Lower my screen brightness to a comfortable minimum
  • Download videos instead of streaming them repeatedly
  • Unsubscribe from emails I never read to reduce stored data
  • Walk, bike, or take public transit instead of asking for a ride
