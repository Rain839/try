Introduction

What is Green ICT?

Green Information and Communication Technology, or Green ICT, refers to the environmentally responsible use, management, and disposal of technology. It is an action, a movement of people who want innovation without harming the health of people or the environment.

Why is environmental sustainability important?

Earth's environmental assets are inherently finite. Human activities contribute to pollution, climate change, and the decline of raw materials. It is a necessity to ensure that technological advancement does not create unnecessary environmental damage. ICT can contribute to sustainability, but its production, disposal, and operation can also cause environmental problems.

How does technology affect the environment?

The impact can be both positive and negative. Digital technology can reduce paper use, improve energy management, and let us do things more efficiently. In contrast, producing and operating electronic devices requires energy and raw materials, while discarded devices contribute to electronic waste. Technology needs to be developed, used, and disposed of responsibly, which is exactly what Green ICT is made for.

Understanding Green ICT

In our modern world, technological advancement has solved many problems over the decades, but these same advancements create problems as well. These problems connect to the surrounding environment, people's health, and industries. From this tension emerges Green ICT: the act of designing, using, reusing, recycling, or creating technology that is not harmful to the environment or the people around you.

What are its goals?

Green ICT follows a set of goals that we are still striving to achieve:

  1. Reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions from devices, infrastructure, vehicles, and anything that emits carbon. Large quantities of emissions and rising energy consumption are dangerous for both people and the environment.
  2. Minimize electronic waste by prolonging the lifespan of devices and enhancing their quality. More durable electronics mean less e-waste.
  3. Decrease the use of harmful materials and chemicals and manage finite resources smartly in manufacturing. Resources are finite, but managed efficiently they can last a long time.
  4. Use digital tools to promote and protect the Green ICT campaign, environmental protection, and other sustainability goals.

Why is it important?

As civilization and technology advance, new problems emerge alongside the solutions. The ICT sector alone produces approximately 2 to 3% of global carbon emissions, and that excludes other sectors. Every use of electronic devices creates a carbon footprint, and as a digital user grows, so does their digital footprint. Green ICT matters because it protects not only us but the environment. But a campaign needs its people: the more who join, the more people, things, and environments are saved and managed properly.

Benefits of Green ICT

Green ICT helps the environment by making technology more eco-friendly and responsible. Using energy-efficient computers and devices reduces electricity use and pollution. It encourages people to reuse, repair, and recycle instead of simply throwing things away.

It can also reduce paper use: files, communication, and many tasks can be done digitally. Online meetings reduce the need to travel, which lowers carbon emissions. Overall, Green ICT shows that technology can be useful while still caring for the environment.

Green ICT reduces energy consumption by optimizing power output and practicing responsible gadget use, putting computers to sleep, choosing low-power components, and unplugging chargers when devices are fully charged.

It reduces electronic waste by encouraging people to repair, reuse, upgrade, and properly recycle electronic devices. By responsibly using computers, phones, printers, and other equipment, we extend their lifespan and reduce the number of devices unnecessarily thrown away.

Examples of Green ICT Practices

Renewable energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into clean electricity with zero gas emissions. Wind turbines use moving air to generate power without burning fuels, creating no harmful air pollution.

Sustainable transportation

Electric vehicles (cars, buses, and trucks) run on batteries instead of gas, releasing no exhaust fumes. Biking, walking, or taking public transit further cuts carbon footprints.

Waste management

Waste-to-energy turns trash into electricity, heat, and fuel. It makes use of an otherwise wasted resource, reduces landfilling, and creates opportunities for resource recovery.

Climate innovations

Carbon capture traps CO₂ directly from factory pipes or the air and stores it. It helps reduce heavy-industry emissions, creates useful products from waste, and supports green jobs.

Our note

Carbon capture sounded like science fiction when we first read about it. It is real, but it is still very expensive and only used at a small scale. We do not think it is a substitute for cutting emissions in the first place.

Green ICT in schools

Schools can use technology in a way that's good for the environment. They can save electricity by turning off computers and projectors when nobody is using them. They can cut paper waste by giving assignments and making announcements digitally. Old computers and batteries should be fixed, reused, or recycled, reducing e-waste. By teaching students and teachers to use technology responsibly, schools protect the environment while still benefiting from modern tools.

What students can do

  • Power management: Turn off computers, monitors, and printers when not in use; enable sleep modes; unplug chargers when fully charged.
  • Reduce paper waste: Use digital notes, submit assignments online, read e-books; print double-sided only when necessary.
  • Extend device lifespan: Take care of laptops and phones; repair instead of replacing; avoid unnecessary upgrades.
  • Proper e-waste disposal: Never throw old batteries, chargers, or gadgets in the trash; drop them at designated recycling centers.

Conclusion

Why should everyone support Green ICT?

Technology is an important part of modern life. By being responsible with the technology we have, we can reduce energy consumption, electronic waste, and resource use, while still enjoying the benefits technology brings. Its environmental impact should not be ignored when used improperly.

Simple actions you can start today

Turn off unused devices. Use power-saving modes. Reduce unnecessary printing. Take care of the electronics you own. Recycle old electronics properly. These actions are simple, but practicing them daily helps create a more environmentally responsible and sustainable future for all of us.
